# Star Trek ship guide

A free, printable guide to 18 well-known starships from across the Star Trek series and films: 4 pages, A4 landscape. An at-a-glance table sorted into four colour groups (Starfleet, Klingon, Romulan, other powers), a card for every ship (registry or class, two field notes, captain or crew, story era, where you can see it and a fun fact), a real-world timeline of the shows and films, and a notes page.

Built by MSTR BLDR on **AiRT Official**. An unofficial fan reference, written fresh in our own words. There are no ship pictures: the only graphic is our own simple timeline of bars and dots.

## Pages

1. Starships at a glance: all 18 ships in one table, colour key, ship names
2. Starfleet ships (one card per ship)
3. Klingon, Romulan and other powers (one card per ship)
4. Real-world timeline, notes and sources

Or download the files from this repo and print the PDF at 100% on A4 landscape, in colour. You can also open `star-trek-ship-guide.html` in a modern browser and print from there.

## Files

- `star-trek-ship-guide.pdf`: the ship guide. Print at 100% on A4 landscape.
- `star-trek-ship-guide.html`: the source. Chrome lays out the table, cards and timeline, checks nothing spills off a page, gets cut off or overlaps, and prints the PDF. The finished PDF has no scripts, links or form fields; fonts are embedded (Exo 2 and Barlow, both open-licence fonts).
- `index.html`: the landing page with a page-by-page preview (`pages/`).
- `SOURCES.md`: where every fact was checked.

## Checks

- Every ship was checked by script against its Memory Alpha (Star Trek wiki) ship and class pages: registry, class, type, captain or crew, story era, the shows or films it appears in, and the key words of each field note and fun fact. Air dates were checked against Wikipedia (0 problems).
- Every page rendered and looked at; an automatic check finds anything that spills past the page edge, into the footer, is clipped or overlaps (0 found). The printed table, cards and timeline were read back and compared with the data (18 of 18; timeline bars at the right years).
- All wording is our own. No ship pictures, logos, insignia, stills, official art or official fonts. The colours are our own palette.

## The ASTARR prompt used

PROBLEM: My family loves Star Trek, but with so many ships across so many series and films, we can never remember which ship is which, who flew it, or where we saw it.

ASTARR PROMPT

ACT AS: A Star Trek fan historian and information designer who makes clear, printable reference guides for families.

SPECIFIC TOPIC: A Star Trek ship guide covering 18 well-known starships from across the series and films, from Starfleet ships like the Enterprise and Voyager to Klingon, Romulan, Borg, Ferengi, Cardassian and Dominion ships. Write it all in your own words, and mark it as an unofficial fan reference: no logos, insignia, stills, official art or official fonts.

ACTIONS: Make an at-a-glance table of all 18 with what each ship is, its class, its best-known captain or crew, when it flies in the stories and which show or film you can see it in. Add a simple colour key that sorts them into four groups: Starfleet, Klingon, Romulan and other powers. Add a card for each ship with its registry or class, two field notes and one fun fact. Add a real-world timeline of the shows and films and a notes page. Check every fact against trusted sources like Memory Alpha and Wikipedia.

RESULT&RULES: I want a printable A4 landscape PDF of about 4 pages, with the colour key on every page. Easy to read, family-friendly, and marked as an unofficial fan reference that isn't affiliated with Paramount or CBS.

ASTARR = Act As, Specific Topic, Actions, Result, Rules.
