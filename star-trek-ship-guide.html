Ship names

Starfleet ships carry a registry number like NCC-1701. Later ships named Enterprise add a letter, like the Enterprise-D and Enterprise-E in this guide.

Starfleet ships

One card per ship: registry and class, two field notes, the captain, when it flies in the stories, where you can see it, and one fun fact.

Colour keyStarfleet Klingon Romulan Other powers

Enterprise

Starfleet
Registry NX-01 · NX class explorer
Field notes
  • The first Earth ship built around a warp 5 engine.
  • Launched in 2151 and retired in 2161.
Captain Jonathan Archer
Era 22nd century
Seen in Enterprise (2001–2005)
Fun fact: In real life, its class shares names with the first space shuttles.

USS Enterprise

Starfleet
Registry NCC-1701 · Constitution class
Field notes
  • Famous for its five-year missions of exploration.
  • Christopher Pike was captain before Kirk.
Captain James T. Kirk (Pike before him)
Era 23rd century
Seen in The Original Series (1966–1969)
Fun fact: Kirk set it to blow itself up so Klingons couldn't take it.

USS Excelsior

Starfleet
Registry NCC-2000 · Excelsior class
Field notes
  • The first Starfleet ship fitted with transwarp drive.
  • Later won fame under Captain Sulu.
Captain Hikaru Sulu
Era 23rd century
Seen in The Search for Spock (1984)
Fun fact: Scotty sabotaged it so it couldn't chase Kirk.

USS Enterprise-D

Starfleet
Registry NCC-1701-D · Galaxy class explorer
Field notes
  • The saucer could separate from the rest of the ship.
  • Families, including children, lived on board.
Captain Jean-Luc Picard
Era 24th century
Seen in The Next Generation (1987–1994)
Fun fact: It crash-landed on the planet Veridian III in 2371.

USS Defiant

Starfleet
Registry NX-74205 · Defiant class escort
Field notes
  • Designed in response to the Borg threat.
  • Only about 50 crew, on just five decks.
Captain Benjamin Sisko
Era 24th century
Seen in Deep Space Nine (1993–1999)
Fun fact: The Romulans lent it a cloaking device to use.

USS Voyager

Starfleet
Registry NCC-74656 · Intrepid class explorer
Field notes
  • Swept 70,000 light years into the Delta Quadrant.
  • Packed with new bio-neural circuitry.
Captain Kathryn Janeway
Era 24th century
Seen in Voyager (1995–2001)
Fun fact: One of the few Starfleet ships that could land on a planet.

USS Enterprise-E

Starfleet
Registry NCC-1701-E · Sovereign class
Field notes
  • Seen as the best of Starfleet ship design.
  • Launched from the San Francisco Fleet Yards.
Captain Jean-Luc Picard
Era 24th century
Seen in First Contact (1996)
Fun fact: Its crew followed the Borg back in time to the year 2063.

USS Discovery

Starfleet
Registry NCC-1031 · Crossfield class
Field notes
  • Built from the ground up to test the spore drive.
  • Its sister ship was the USS Glenn.
Captain Captains Lorca, Pike, Saru and Burnham
Era 23rd and 32nd centuries
Seen in Discovery (2017–2024)
Fun fact: It travelled to the 32nd century and was refitted there.

USS Cerritos

Starfleet
Registry NCC-75567 · California class
Field notes
  • Handles second contact, after the first meeting.
  • Its stories follow the junior crew.
Captain Carol Freeman
Era 24th century
Seen in Lower Decks (2020–2024)
Fun fact: It once took Voyager, by then a museum ship, home to Earth.

1 Story time or real time?

Story time and real time don't match. The Enterprise NX-01 flies in the 2150s, about a century before Kirk, but its series first aired in 2001.

2 So many Enterprises

Kirk's NCC-1701 came first in real life. Picard commanded two more: the Enterprise-D on TV, then the Enterprise-E in the films.

3 Cloaking devices

Romulan and Klingon ships could turn invisible with a cloaking device. Starfleet's Defiant carried one on loan from the Romulans.

4 Lost far from home

Voyager was swept 70,000 light years away. At top speed, the trip home was first expected to take about 75 years.

5 Second contact

First contact is the famous first meeting with a new people. The Cerritos does the next step: the follow-up visit.

6 Fan reference

Registries, classes and captains come from the shows and films. Where sources disagree, we use Memory Alpha, the Star Trek wiki.
